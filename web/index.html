Under the bonnet

It solves the picture, not six colours.

1. Live capture

The scan order establishes the six fixed-centre faces. Each face is split into 3×3 image tiles after capture.

2. Python reconstruction

A Pyodide worker compares border continuity while tracking the rigid 3D orientation of each physical edge and corner cubie.

3. Cube constraints

One-to-one assignments are constrained by edge flip, corner twist and permutation parity, so visually plausible but impossible states are rejected.

4. Solve and animate

A two-phase solver produces the turns. Picture-centre orientation is tracked separately and corrected after the ordinary cubies are solved.
